# EIKB-C-59 | ALS iPSC 药物发现

- **标题**: Therapeutic frontiers in ALS: iPSC-based drug discovery, cell therapy, and gene therapy
- **来源**: PMID 42359165 · PMC13292465 · 2026 · 综述
